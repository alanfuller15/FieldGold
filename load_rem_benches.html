Load REM Terrace Candidates

Adds 20 REM-derived bench candidates to your FieldGold shared record, each carrying its DNR land status. As of 2026-07-25 every one of the 20 has been checked; none are left unknown.

Honest about what these are: geomorphically-derived terrace candidates — flat ground at bench height above the real river, extracted by the published REM / TerEx method from lidar. They are NOT confirmed benches and NOT gold. The Little Su's gold is likely morainal (glacial), which a REM can't distinguish from sorted fluvial terraces. These are first-pass targets to go pan — the ground makes the call.
All 20 are now land-status checked. 12 of them are not ground to work. This list used to be ranked by terrace area alone, which put never-checked ground at #1. It is now ranked by land status first, geometry second. 8 of 20 came back clean — but two of those eight (REM-7 and REM-19) are outside the Hatcher Pass Public Use Area and so do not get the PUA's recreational-panning permission. The other 12 are flagged AVOID: one inside Mineral Closing Order 549, six inside LLO 5, five inside pending application ADL 229824.

Two of the eight clean are checked more deeply than the other six. REM-2, REM-13 and REM-7 went through a full 16-layer battery on 2026-07-21. REM-1, REM-5, REM-11, REM-19 and REM-20 went through an 8-layer Tier-1 battery on 2026-07-25 — every one of those CLEAN calls was re-fetched independently rather than taken on trust, but eight rarer layers (native allotment, Mental Health Trust, federal action, easement, land disposal, management agreement, agreement/settlement, restricted-use authorization) were not run on any of them. That is why the shallower five rank below the deeper three even where their geometry is better.

Safe to re-run: entries upsert by id, so this replaces any older copies already on your device rather than duplicating them. If you loaded the old unranked list before, running this once fixes it.

Two questions still open — neither is answered by this page.
  • No bench sits inside a state mining claim — but claims are near. All 20 points were queried against DNR ME112 (active state mining claims) and all 20 came back empty. That is the register that governs this ground; the BLM federal layer on the map is not. What is not known is how near the nearest claim is: there are 22 active claims in the reach and 3 in the box holding REM-1, REM-11, REM-19 and REM-20. Proximity was never measured. Do not read "no claim on the point" as "nothing around you."
  • Claims can be staked at any time. The clean result is dated 2026-07-21. Re-run the claim check the week you travel; a bench that was clear in July can be staked in August.
  • LLO 5 and MCO 549 govern mineral-RIGHTS acquisition. Whether they also bar recreational hand panning inside the Public Use Area is unresolved — DNR's PUA fact sheet allows panning "anywhere within the boundaries... except for several valid mining claims." This now blocks six candidates: REM-3, REM-4, REM-10, REM-12, REM-15 and REM-18. Put REM-18 to DNR Division of Mining, Land & Water (907-269-8600) specifically — it is inside LLO 5 and inside the PUA, so it is the one point whose answer settles all six.
  • A pending application is not a closure. REM-8, REM-9, REM-16 and REM-17 are clean on every mineral layer and inside the PUA. Their only hit is ADL 229824 (Fishhook Renewable Energy LLC), status "ADDTL INFO REQUESTED (10)" — a pending industrial/commercial lease application. They are flagged AVOID on exposure alone, for consistency with REM-6, not because the ground is closed. Ask DMLW about pending applications on the same call: if the answer is permissive, REM-8 (36,644 m², 5.2 m above the channel) belongs near the top of this list, not near the bottom.
